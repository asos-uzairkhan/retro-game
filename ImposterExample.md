## Hints

The imposter has never worked at a nuclear power plant.
The imposter has never been hired to solve a mystery.
The imposter did not attend a school for the magically gifted.
The imposter does not patrol a city fighting crime after dark.
The imposter is not an animal with oversized, round ears.
The imposter has never lived in a swamp.
The imposter cannot cling to walls or swing between buildings.
The imposter's sentences follow normal word order.
The imposter does not work as a cook under the sea.
The imposter cannot create ice or snow at will.
The imposter did not build their own equipment using personal wealth and engineering skill.
The imposter carries no staff and casts no spells.
The imposter has no particular fondness for honey.
The imposter does not work for a government intelligence agency.
The imposter carries no lasso and was not raised on a hidden island.
The imposter is not a toy that has come to life.
The imposter's motives are not rooted in chaos for its own sake.
The imposter is of ordinary or larger-than-average height, not notably small.
The imposter has never captained a ship on the high seas.
