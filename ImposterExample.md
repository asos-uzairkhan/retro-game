# Imposter Example

## Suspect List

1. Harry Potter (*Harry Potter*)
2. Hermione Granger (*Harry Potter*)
3. Elsa (*Frozen*)
4. Peter Parker / Spider-Man (*Marvel*)
5. Shrek (*Shrek*)
6. Pikachu (*Pokémon*)
7. Mickey Mouse (*Disney*)
8. Cinderella (*Cinderella*)

These 8 were picked because each one cleanly sits on one side of several obvious axes (human vs. non-human, male vs. female, has powers vs. doesn't, fairy-tale kingdom vs. not, etc.), which makes it easy to write hints that eliminate a clean group each time.

## Selected Imposter

**Peter Parker / Spider-Man**

## Hints

Each hint is a true statement about the imposter that rules out a handful of suspects (never just one, never all of them).

| # | Hint (written by the imposter) | Rules out |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "I'm fully human — no animal biology, ogre features, or magical-creature traits." | Shrek, Pikachu, Mickey Mouse |
| 2 | "I'm male." | Hermione, Elsa, Cinderella |
| 3 | "I have real superhuman or magical abilities — it's not just a costume or a title." | Shrek, Mickey Mouse, Cinderella |
| 4 | "I don't cast spells with a wand, and I wasn't born into some magical bloodline." | Harry Potter, Hermione Granger |
| 5 | "Nobody in my story calls me 'Your Majesty' — I don't wear a crown." | Elsa, Cinderella |
| 6 | "There's no castle or fairy-tale kingdom anywhere in my story." | Elsa, Cinderella, Shrek |
| 7 | "I don't have a signature animal sidekick following me around." | Harry Potter (owl), Hermione Granger (cat), Cinderella (mice), Shrek (Donkey) |
| 8 | "I'm still a teenager, going to school every day with people my own age." | Elsa, Shrek, Pikachu, Mickey Mouse, Cinderella |
| 9 | "I lost my parents young and was raised by a different relative instead." | Hermione Granger, Elsa, Shrek, Pikachu, Mickey Mouse |
| 10 | "My home has real streets, buildings, and traffic — not a swamp or a wild route." | Shrek, Pikachu |
| 11 | "I rely on some kind of tool or gadget to use my abilities — it isn't 100% just my body." | Elsa, Shrek, Pikachu, Mickey Mouse, Cinderella |
| 12 | "I don't have any siblings — step or otherwise." | Elsa (sister Anna), Cinderella (stepsisters) |

Every hint above is true of **Peter Parker**, so he's never ruled out by his own clues — but each one knocks out a few of the other 7 suspects, and by hint 12 he's the only one left standing.
